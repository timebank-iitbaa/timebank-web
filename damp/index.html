What is DAMP

A department programme, not a one-off favour.

The Department Academic Mentorship Programme (DAMP) is a student-run initiative under the aegis of IIT Bombay's Student Mentorship Programme (SMP). It's designed primarily to support students transitioning from their first year into department-specific academics.

Every sophomore is assigned a senior mentor from their own department, to help them navigate courses, electives, and academic or personal challenges. DAMP also supports students in later years who face significant academic difficulties or backlogs — so every student has a reliable senior support system within the department, and academically struggling students have a way back on track.

DAMP functions under the guidance of the Institute SMP leadership and the respective department's faculty coordination structure.

In Chemical Engineering

How the department team is structured

Each department's DAMP is overseen by Department Academic Mentorship Programme Coordinators (DAMPCs) — typically fourth-year students who have previously served as DAMP mentors themselves. The DAMPCs select and coordinate the department's DAMP mentors.

Each mentor is assigned a group of sophomore students — usually around six — whom they support throughout the year.

In Chemical Engineering, the DAMP team also serves as the bridge between students and alumni mentors, facilitating and coordinating the alumni-student mentorship programme — the "+" you see across this site.

The Route — Alumni Mentorship

How the alumni sign-up runs

Once you sign up, here's how the DAMP team takes it from there.

01

Sign up

Alumni register as mentors through the form on this page. Sophomores are assigned a DAMP mentor automatically; no sign-up needed on their end.

02

Get matched

The DAMP team allots each mentee an alumni mentor based on domain, goals and availability.

03

Meet & map the fog

Pairs run regular check-ins — career questions, coursework doubts, or just "what now?" conversations.

04

See the road clearly

By programme end, mentees leave with direction, a contact, and one fewer question mark hanging over them.
