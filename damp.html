THE CURRENT PROGRAMME

What DAMP already does

DAMP — the Department Academic Mentoring Programme — pairs every second-year Chemical Engineering student with third- and fourth-year seniors: someone a little further along to turn to for course choices, workload and campus life.

THE ALUMNI LAYER

What the "+" adds

DAMP+ is that layer added on top of DAMP: Alumni bring the industry perspective a peer mentor can't — real-world experience, choices, and career paths. The Alumni TimeBank turns alumni time into structured contribution — pledged, matched to a real need, and followed through to impact.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered

Do I need to currently work in a traditional chemical industry?

No. What matters is that Chemical Engineering shaped your path in some way — core process roles, adjacent technical sectors, or a non-core path you moved into afterwards all count.

Do I have to be based in Mumbai?

No. Please express your interest even if you are not based in Mumbai. The department will try to arrange for Guest House stay (subject to availability).

Will I be assigned individual students?

Not for this evening — it's small-group conversations, not one-on-one assignments. Any longer-term matching would be discussed separately, with no obligation.

What happens after I express interest?

You will first share your background, then indicate whether you are available for the 9 October 2026 session. The TimeBank Office will follow up about this session or a future opportunity that fits your interests and availability. No ongoing commitment is assumed.
